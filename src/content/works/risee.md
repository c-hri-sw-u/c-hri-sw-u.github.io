Risee started as a selection-translation app that keeps a history of the words I meet on my computer. It grew into a way to run any LLM "action" on selected text: translate, polish, summarize, or a custom prompt. The first version was macOS native (summer 2025); in January 2026 I rebuilt it in Electron over a weekend, with a ring of capsules that orbits the selected text. I use it every day.

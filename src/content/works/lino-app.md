Lino is an AI canvas for visual thinking — an infinite, spatial workspace for your ideas, notes, and research, with an AI agent that acts directly on them. It is designed to preserve human context, offering a minimalist visual playground to shape raw ideas into reality. Stop building static workflows and copying notes back and forth. Lino replaces fragmented tools, providing a unified thinking and working partner that understands your ideas and executes complex tasks natively.

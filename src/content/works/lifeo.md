An iOS app for people living in a language that is not their own. Translation that understands context, plus three pages that make it stick: History (a timeline of what you learned), Review (AI picks up your recent interests and serves cards about them) and Map (where you learned each phrase). I built a first version, then redesigned it from scratch in the iOS 26 design language.
